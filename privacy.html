Ruta KM0 Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 · Effective November 1, 2026 · Leer en español

This policy explains what information the Ruta KM0 iPhone app handles, where it is stored, who can see it and how to delete it. Ruta KM0 is an internal tool of Transportes Ruta KM0, a small freight company in Ecuador, used to keep track of the trips, expenses, driver hours, cash and receivables of its cargo vehicles. The Spanish version is the reference text; this is a faithful translation.

In short

  • Everything you enter in the app is stored only on your iPhone.
  • The app has no user accounts, no servers of its own, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tools that collect data.
  • Transportes Ruta KM0, as the app developer, does not receive and cannot see the information you enter.
  • The app uses the camera and photos only to attach receipts.
  • You decide when to share a receipt, a monthly report or a backup, and with whom.
  • You can delete everything from within the app or by uninstalling it.

Transportes Ruta KM0, Camilo Ponce Enríquez, Azuay province, Ecuador.

2.Information the app handles

The app stores only what the user types in or attaches:

  • Business and vehicle: license plate, vehicle name, monthly loan payment and due date, receipt and VAT settings.
  • Owners and staff: names, national ID number, mobile number, email, address, ownership share, hours worked, payments, contributions and withdrawals.
  • Customers: name or business name, national ID or tax ID (RUC), address, mobile number, email and credit terms.
  • Operations: freight trips (origin, destination, cargo, amount and payment method), expenses, collections, partial payments, cash movements, and daily and monthly closings.
  • Photos of invoices, receipts or of the service, which the user chooses to attach.
  • Device preferences, such as the “records” or “review only” mode.

The app does not access your location, contacts, calendar, microphone, health data, advertising identifier, or any usage or diagnostic data.

3.Where it is stored

  • In the app’s private storage on your iPhone (data files and photos). iOS isolates that storage from other apps and, if the iPhone has a passcode, protects it with device encryption.
  • If you use iCloud Backup or back up your iPhone to a computer, iOS includes this data in that backup, under your own Apple Account. Transportes Ruta KM0 has no access to those backups.
  • The app works without an internet connection and does not send the information to any server.

4.What the app does not do

  • It does not ask you to create an account or sign in.
  • It has no servers of its own and does not send data to Transportes Ruta KM0 or to third parties.
  • It contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party SDKs.
  • It does not sell, rent or transfer personal data.
  • It does not build profiles or make automated decisions about people.
  • It does not process payments.

5.iPhone permissions

  • Camera: to photograph invoices and receipts. iOS asks for your permission the first time; you can withdraw it at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Camera.
  • Photos: to choose saved images and attach them as receipts. The app uses the iOS photo picker, which gives the app only the images you select, not your whole photo library.

Without these permissions the app still works; you just cannot attach photos.

6.When information leaves the phone

The app generates receipts, vouchers, the monthly closing package (PDF, Excel and photos) and backup files (a ZIP with data and photos). These files leave the phone only when you tap “Share” and pick a destination in the iOS share sheet (for example WhatsApp, Files, Mail or AirDrop), or when you print them via AirPrint. From then on, the copy you sent is subject to the rules of the service or person you chose.

Backup files are not password-protected: keep them in a safe place and send them only to people you trust.

7.iCloud sync (upcoming version)

An upcoming version will let the owners sync data between their iPhones using Apple’s iCloud (CloudKit):

  • Data will be stored in the private iCloud database of the owner who turns on sync, under their own Apple Account.
  • That owner will be able to share it by invitation with other people, with read-only or read-and-write permission, and revoke access at any time.
  • Apple runs that infrastructure and handles the data under its privacy policy. iCloud servers may be located outside Ecuador.
  • Transportes Ruta KM0, as the developer, will have no access to that data: there is no intermediate server of its own.

If anything described here changes when that feature ships, this policy will be updated first.

8.Purpose and legal basis

The information is recorded to run the transport business: issue receipts, track collections and credit, pay staff, keep accounts among the owners and meet accounting and tax obligations. Customer and staff data are processed on the basis of the contractual relationship (transport service or employment) and compliance with legal obligations, under Ecuador’s Organic Law on Personal Data Protection (Ley Orgánica de Protección de Datos Personales, Official Registry, Fifth Supplement No. 459, May 26, 2021) and its General Regulation. Only the data needed for those purposes is recorded.

9.Retention

Data stays on the iPhone until you delete it or uninstall the app. Transportes Ruta KM0 keeps the records of its operations for as long as needed to run the business and as required by Ecuadorian accounting and tax rules.

10.How to correct or delete data

  • Correct: edit the record in the app (for example, Clientes › Editar).
  • Delete everything in the app: Más › Datos y respaldo › “Borrar todos los datos de este teléfono” (Delete all data on this phone). It removes trips, expenses, collections, work hours, customers and photos, and resets the app to a freshly installed state. It asks for double confirmation and cannot be undone.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all of its data and photos from the iPhone.
  • Copies you shared (WhatsApp, Files, email) and iPhone backups are deleted from those same places.
  • Once iCloud sync is available, the owner will be able to stop sharing and delete the data from their iCloud from within the app.

11.Your rights

Under Ecuador’s Organic Law on Personal Data Protection, anyone whose data is recorded in the app (customers, drivers, owners) may exercise the rights of information, access, rectification and updating, erasure, objection, portability, suspension of processing, and not to be subject to decisions based solely on automated processing.

To exercise them, contact Transportes Ruta KM0 on WhatsApp at +593 98 611 8538 or by email at jefferson.huanca@icloud.com with your name, your national ID or RUC number and your request. We may ask you to confirm your identity. We will reply within 15 days at most. If you get no reply or are not satisfied, you may file a complaint with Ecuador’s Superintendency of Personal Data Protection (Superintendencia de Protección de Datos Personales).

12.Security

Protection depends mainly on the phone. We recommend using a passcode and Face ID, keeping iOS up to date and storing backups in a safe place.

13.Children

Ruta KM0 is a work tool for adults. It is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

14.This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, forms or analytics of its own. GitHub may log technical data about visits, such as IP addresses, under its privacy statement.

15.Changes to this policy

If the app changes how it handles data (for example, when iCloud sync is turned on), we will publish the new version on this page with its effective date and mention it in the App Store update notes.
