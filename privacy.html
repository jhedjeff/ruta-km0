Ruta KM0 Privacy Policy

Policy version 1.2 · Effective November 2, 2026 · Applies to the Ruta KM0 app 1.0 and 1.1 · Leer en español

This policy explains what information the Ruta KM0 iPhone app handles, where it is stored, who can see it and how to delete it. Ruta KM0 is an internal tool of Transportes Ruta KM0, a small freight company in Ecuador, used to keep track of the trips, expenses, driver hours, cash and receivables of its cargo vehicles. The Spanish version is the reference text; this is a faithful translation.

In short

  • Everything you enter in the app is stored on your iPhone.
  • Starting with version 1.1, if the data owner turns on shared iCloud, the information is also stored in their own iCloud account (an Apple service) and is visible only to the people they invite, with the permission they choose.
  • The app has no accounts of its own, no servers of its own, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tools that collect data.
  • Transportes Ruta KM0, as the app developer, does not receive and cannot see the information you enter, except as an invited person to that data.
  • The app uses the iOS photo picker only to attach receipts: it sees only the photos you choose.
  • You decide when to share a receipt, a monthly report or a backup, and with whom.
  • You can delete everything from within the app or by uninstalling it.

2.Information the app handles

The app stores only what the user types in or attaches:

  • Business and vehicle: license plate, vehicle name, monthly loan payment and due date, receipt and VAT settings.
  • Owners and staff: names, national ID number, mobile number, email, address, ownership share, hours worked, payments, contributions and withdrawals.
  • Customers: name or business name, national ID or tax ID (RUC), address, mobile number, email and credit terms.
  • Operations: freight trips (origin, destination, cargo, amount and payment method), expenses, collections, partial payments, cash movements, and daily and monthly closings.
  • Photos of invoices, receipts or of the service, which the user chooses to attach.
  • Device preferences, such as the “records” or “review only” mode.
  • With shared iCloud: the name and email or phone number of the invited people’s Apple Accounts and their permission, which iCloud shows to those sharing the data.

The app does not access your location, contacts, calendar, microphone, health data, advertising identifier, or any usage or diagnostic data.

3.Where it is stored

  • In the app’s private storage on your iPhone (data files and photos). iOS isolates that storage from other apps and, if the iPhone has a passcode, protects it with device encryption.
  • If you use iCloud Backup or back up your iPhone to a computer, iOS includes this data in that backup, under your own Apple Account. Transportes Ruta KM0 has no access to those backups.
  • With shared iCloud turned on (version 1.1 and later), a copy of the records and photos is stored in the data owner’s iCloud account, in a private area of the app that only they and their invitees can open. It uses their iCloud storage. The content of each record is kept in an end-to-end encrypted iCloud field; photos are stored with Apple’s iCloud encryption.
  • The app works without an internet connection. Apart from iCloud, when you turn it on, it does not send the information to any server.

4.What the app does not do

  • It does not ask you to create an account or sign in: shared iCloud uses the Apple Account already on the iPhone.
  • It has no servers of its own and does not send data to Transportes Ruta KM0 or to third parties. iCloud is an Apple service that you use with your own account.
  • It contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party SDKs.
  • It does not sell, rent or transfer personal data.
  • It does not build profiles or make automated decisions about people.
  • It does not process payments.

5.iPhone permissions

  • Photos: to choose saved images and attach them as receipts. The app uses the iOS photo picker, which gives the app only the images you select; it has no access to your whole photo library and does not need to ask for it.
  • Camera: the app does not use the camera. To attach a receipt, take a picture with the iPhone Camera app and then pick it from the photo picker.
  • iCloud: with shared iCloud, the app uses the iPhone’s iCloud sign-in and receives silent notices from Apple when something changes; it shows no notifications and does not ask for permission to do so.

The app does not ask for location, contacts, microphone or notification permissions.

6.When information leaves the phone

The app generates receipts, vouchers, the monthly closing package (PDF, Excel and photos) and backup files (a ZIP with data and photos). These files leave the phone only when you tap “Share” and pick a destination in the iOS share sheet (for example WhatsApp, Files, Mail or AirDrop), or when you print them via AirPrint. From then on, the copy you sent is subject to the rules of the service or person you chose.

Backup files are not password-protected: keep them in a safe place and send them only to people you trust.

With shared iCloud turned on, each record and photo is also sent, automatically and encrypted, to the data owner’s iCloud account, and from there to the invited people’s iPhones. Before replacing a phone’s data with the data in iCloud, the app saves an automatic backup on that phone.

7.Sync with shared iCloud

Version 1.0 does not sync: each iPhone keeps its own copy, and data moves from one phone to another only through a backup that you choose to share.

Starting with version 1.1 there is an optional shared iCloud feature:

  • Only one person, the data owner, turns it on in Más › Sincronización. The data is stored in their iCloud account.
  • The owner invites other people with Apple’s sharing screen (via Messages, WhatsApp or their Apple Account email) and chooses each person’s permission: “Can edit” (records) or “Can view and download” (review only). Each invitee opens the invitation with their own Apple Account; the app creates no accounts and asks for no passwords.
  • Only the owner and the people they invite can see the data. The owner can change permissions, remove a person or stop sharing at any time from the same screen.
  • Without signal the app keeps working; changes are sent when the connection returns.
  • Once turned on, sync cannot be turned off from the app. If the owner removes a person or stops sharing, that phone stops receiving changes and keeps a local copy.
  • Sync tasks also run in the background, when iOS allows it, to send and receive changes without opening the app.

8.Purpose and legal basis

The information is recorded to run the transport business: issue receipts, track collections and credit, pay staff, keep accounts among the owners and meet accounting and tax obligations. Customer and staff data are processed on the basis of the contractual relationship (transport service or employment) and compliance with legal obligations, under Ecuador’s Organic Law on Personal Data Protection (Ley Orgánica de Protección de Datos Personales, Official Registry, Fifth Supplement No. 459, May 26, 2021) and its General Regulation. Only the data needed for those purposes is recorded.

9.Retention

Data stays on the iPhone until you delete it or uninstall the app. The iCloud copy stays in the data owner’s account until they delete it. Transportes Ruta KM0 keeps the records of its operations for as long as needed to run the business and as required by Ecuadorian accounting and tax rules.

10.How to correct or delete data

  • Correct: edit the record in the app (for example, Clientes › Editar).
  • Delete everything in the app: Más › Datos y respaldo › “Borrar todos los datos de este teléfono” (Delete all data on this phone). It removes trips, expenses, collections, work hours, customers and photos, and resets the app to a freshly installed state. It asks for double confirmation and cannot be undone.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all of its data and photos from the iPhone. It does not delete the iCloud copy.
  • Deleting the iCloud copy (data owner only): on the iPhone, Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Storage › Ruta KM0 › Delete Data. This deletes it for every invitee. To stop someone from seeing the data without deleting it, go to Más › Sincronización › “Invitar o cambiar permisos” and remove them or stop sharing.
  • While shared iCloud is on, “Borrar todos los datos de este teléfono” is hidden so that nobody deletes everyone’s data by mistake.
  • Copies you shared (WhatsApp, Files, email) and iPhone backups are deleted from those same places.

11.Your rights

Under Ecuador’s Organic Law on Personal Data Protection, anyone whose data is recorded in the app (customers, drivers, owners) may exercise the rights of information, access, rectification and updating, erasure, objection, portability, suspension of processing, and not to be subject to decisions based solely on automated processing.

To exercise them, contact Transportes Ruta KM0 on WhatsApp at +593 98 611 8538 or by email at jefferson.huanca@icloud.com with your name, your national ID or RUC number and your request. We may ask you to confirm your identity. We will reply within 15 days at most. If you get no reply or are not satisfied, you may file a complaint with Ecuador’s Superintendency of Personal Data Protection (Superintendencia de Protección de Datos Personales).

12.Security

Protection depends mainly on the phone and the Apple Account. We recommend using a passcode and Face ID, keeping iOS up to date, turning on two-factor authentication for the Apple Account, inviting only people you trust and storing backups in a safe place.

13.Children

Ruta KM0 is a work tool for adults. It is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

14.This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, forms or analytics of its own. GitHub may log technical data about visits, such as IP addresses, under its privacy statement.

15.Changes to this policy

If the app changes how it handles data, we will publish the new version on this page with its effective date and mention it in the App Store update notes.
